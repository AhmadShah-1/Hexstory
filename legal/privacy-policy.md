# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This policy explains what Hexstory collects and how it is used.

## What we collect

| Data | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google account ID and email address | Google sign-in | Create and identify your account; contact you about it |
| First name from Google | Google sign-in | Only used once, to suggest a display name. Not stored |
| Display name (any name you choose) | You | Shown publicly next to your contributions |
| Stories, edits, sources, images, chat messages, reports | You | Provide the Service; all but reports are **public** |
| IP address, browser info, timestamps | Automatically | Security, rate limiting, abuse and ban enforcement |
| Session cookie | Automatically | Keep you signed in |

We remove location and camera metadata (EXIF) from uploaded images before storing them.

We do not store your Google profile picture or full name. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell your personal data.

## What is public

Your display name, stories, edits (including full version history), uploaded images and chat messages
are visible to everyone. Your email address is never shown publicly.

## Who we share data with

- Infrastructure providers that host the Service (servers, storage), only to run it
- Google, for sign-in
- Authorities, when required by law or to protect someone's safety

## Retention

- Account data: kept until you delete your account.
- Contributions: kept indefinitely because they are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 and form part of
  the shared history. When you delete your account, your name on them is replaced with
  "Deleted user".
- Chat messages: [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 1 year], then deleted.
- IP and security logs: up to 90 days, longer if tied to an abuse investigation.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you can request access to, correction
of, export of, or deletion of your personal data, and object to processing. Contact
[CONTACT EMAIL]. You can delete your account at any time from your account settings.

## Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an
account, contact us and we will remove it.

We will post updates here and notify users of material changes on the Service.
