# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

This policy explains what Hexstory collects and how it is used. Hexstory is operated from Jersey
City, New Jersey, United States, and is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact:
hexstory.world@gmail.com.

## What we collect

| Data | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google account ID and email address | Google sign-in | Create and identify your account; contact you about it |
| First name from Google | Google sign-in | Only used once, to suggest a display name. Not stored |
| Display name (any name you choose) | You | Shown publicly next to your contributions |
| Stories, edits, timeline developments, corrections, collections, sources, images, reports | You | Provide the Service; all but reports are **public** (see below) |
| Moderation records: reports, requests to ban someone, and moderator actions (such as approvals, removals and bans, with their reasons) | You, reviewers and moderators | Keep the Service safe and make moderation accountable. Visible only to reviewers and moderators |
| IP address, browser info, timestamps | Automatically | Security, rate limiting, abuse and ban enforcement |
| Session cookie | Automatically | Keep you signed in |

We remove location and camera metadata (EXIF) from uploaded images before storing them.

**History near me.** If you use "History near me", your browser asks for permission to share your location.
The location is used only on your device, to find the nearest stories among those already loaded in your
browser. It is never sent to Hexstory or stored by us.

We do not store your Google profile picture or full name. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell your personal data.

## What is public

Your display name, published stories, accepted edits (including the version history), accepted timeline
developments, corrections you propose (with their evidence and outcome), collections you start and uploaded
images are visible to everyone. Your profile page shows your display name, your role (such as Member or
Reviewer) and the date you joined.

Some things are not public:

- The list of everything you've contributed, with counts, is visible only to you and to reviewers and moderators.
- Stories waiting for a reviewer's approval, and edits that are waiting for review or were not accepted, are
  visible only to you and to reviewers and moderators.
- Versions that a reviewer or moderator removed from the history are visible only to reviewers and moderators.

Your email address is never shown publicly.

## People named in stories

Stories are about public events and may name public figures. Stories imported from public sources by
the Hexstory Archive account are written to name only public figures central to an event, not
private individuals.
If a story names you, or says something about you that you believe is wrong, use the Report button
or contact us and we will review it.

## Who we share data with

- Infrastructure providers that host the Service, only to run it: Vercel (servers), Neon (database),
  Cloudflare R2 (image storage and delivery) and, where used, Redis Cloud (rate limits and short-lived
  caches)
- Google, for sign-in
- Komoot's Photon service (OpenStreetMap data), to look up places you type into the map's search box: it
  receives the search text from our server, never your IP address or account
- Stripe and PayPal, only if you choose to make a voluntary contribution from the Support page: you pay on
  their own pages, under their privacy policies, and we don't receive or store your payment details
- Authorities, when required by law or to protect someone's safety

## Where your data is stored

Our servers and database are in the United States (Virginia), and images are delivered through
Cloudflare's global network. If you use the Service from outside the United States, your data is
transferred to and processed in the United States.

## Retention

- Account data: kept until you delete your account.
- Contributions: kept indefinitely because they are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 and form part of
  the shared history. When you delete your account, your name on them is replaced with
  "Deleted user".
- Messages from the live chat Hexstory used to offer: no longer shown to anyone; kept only as moderation
  records. You can ask us to delete yours.
- IP and security logs: up to 90 days, longer if tied to an abuse investigation.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or US state privacy laws
such as California's) you can request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your
personal data, and object to processing. We don't sell personal data or share it for targeted
advertising. Contact
hexstory.world@gmail.com. You can delete your account at any time from your account settings.

## Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an
account, contact us and we will remove it.

## Changes

We will post updates here and notify users of material changes on the Service.
